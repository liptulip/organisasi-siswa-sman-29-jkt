## Brand & Style

The design system bridges dynamic student life with the sleek clarity of modern product design. Designed for an extracurricular ("Ekskul") discovery directory, it avoids childish school motifs in favor of an energetic, high-agency startup aesthetic. It treats extracurricular activities as student ventures—celebrating initiative, leadership, sports, arts, and tech innovation.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Neo-Utility** with **Bento Grid architecture**:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** Pristine surfaces, crisp micro-borders, and disciplined typography.
- **Dynamic Energy:** Punchy electric blue accents and hyper-legible colored category badges inject youth and enthusiasm without compromising structural cleanliness.
- **Tactile Softness:** Generous card radiuses, air-light diffused drop shadows, and responsive micro-interactions invite exploration and sign-ups.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-clean, luminous environment using high-value neutrals anchored by high-impact accents.

### Color Tokens & Usage
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC` - Slate 50):** Off-white base canvas creating subtle distinction against foreground components.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used for cards, modal dialogs, and navigation layers.
- **Primary Accent (`#2563EB` - Blue 600):** Electric blue for primary actions, active filters, selected states, and key navigational focus points.
- **Secondary Accent (`#38BDF8` - Sky 400):** Used for digital badges, technology/STEM categories, and interactive hover highlights.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#10B981` - Emerald 500):** Signifies open recruitment periods, active registrations, and verified badges.
- **Text & Structure Hierarchy:**
  - **Slate 900 (`#0F172A`):** Dominant display titles, primary headings, and bold labels.
  - **Slate 700 (`#334155`):** High-clarity body copy and secondary headers.
  - **Slate 500 (`#64748B`):** Supporting metadata, schedules, timestamps, and input placeholding.
  - **Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline borders, structural card strokes, and subtle dividers.

### Category Color Accents (Pill Badges)
Vibrant category badges use low-opacity tinted backgrounds paired with deep, saturated text:
- **Sports & Athletics:** Coral/Orange tint (`#FFF7ED` / `#EA580C`)
- **Arts & Culture:** Violet tint (`#F5F3FF` / `#7C3AED`)
- **STEM & Robotics:** Sky/Blue tint (`#EFF6FF` / `#1D4ED8`)
- **Leadership & Debate:** Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5` / `#047857`)

## Typography

The pairing of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** anchors the system's identity:
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric curves and wide horizontal proportions bring personality, warmth, and modernity to names, metrics, and hero sections.
- **Body & Labels (Inter):** Industrial-grade legibility for descriptive copy, schedule tables, member requirements, and micro-labels.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize snug letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) for a unified editorial silhouette, while pill badges and auxiliary labels use expanded tracking for maximum legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The system follows a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around **Bento Grid modules**:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width of 1280px, 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters and 24px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px (`1rem`) outer margins.

### Bento Grid Implementation for Detail View
Ekskul profile detail views break away from standard linear pages into an interlocking Bento Grid:
- **Primary Hero Tile:** Spans 8 columns (Desktop) containing the activity highlight video/photo, headline, and lead recruitment CTA.
- **Schedule & Logistics Tile:** Spans 4 columns featuring session days, times, and location markers.
- **Achievements & Stats Tile:** Spans 4 columns highlighting past awards, member count, and faculty advisor.
- **Requirements & Fees Tile:** Spans 4 columns detailing uniform, kit costs, and grade prerequisites.
- **Gallery & Student Work Tile:** Spans 4 columns showcasing photos and project outcomes.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through a low-profile, clean-room approach, pairing hairline borders with ultra-diffuse ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, elevated by a soft ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) with heightened shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Backdrop blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) paired with high diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The design uses a progressive curvature scale tailored to component scale:
- **Base Rounding (0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, tooltips, list items, and mini action buttons.
- **Large Rounding (1rem / 16px):** Form panels, search command bars, and bento child modules.
- **Extra-Large Rounding (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-2xl`):** Primary directory cards, bento grid container blocks, and media showcase tiles.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Category pill badges, filter chips, and primary rounded CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#2563EB` background with white text, font `label-md`, `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (12px), smooth transition on hover (`background-color: #1D4ED8`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#334155`, hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, text `#64748B`, hover text `#0F172A`, hover background `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & Category Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full`), height 28px, padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Text styled with `badge-caps`. Composed with 10% opacity colored backgrounds matched to saturated text (e.g., `#EFF6FF` base with `#1D4ED8` text for STEM).
- **Filter Chips (Directory):** Height 36px, `rounded-full`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, interactive active state sets background `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Bento Grid Tiles
- **Directory Card:** Pure white background, `rounded-2xl` (24px), border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, default ambient shadow. Contains top-edge image/banner with 16:9 aspect ratio, category pill overlay in the top-right, title in `headline-sm`, snippet in `body-md`, and footer containing meeting times and "Join" trigger.
- **Bento Cells:** Enclosed in `rounded-2xl` surfaces with internal padding of `space-lg` (24px).

### Input Fields & Search Command Bar
- **Global Search Bar:** Elevated floating element (`height: 52px`), `rounded-xl`, left-aligned search icon in `#64748B`, placeholder text styled in `body-md`, clear border `#E2E8F0`, focus state with electric blue outline (`ring-2 ring-blue-500/20 border-[#2563EB]`).
- **Form Controls:** 44px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` stroke, radius 8px.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Selection Controls:** 20px x 20px box, `rounded` (6px for checkbox, full circle for radio), border `#CBD5E1`. On check: fill `#2563EB` with pure white check mark icon.

### Status Indicators
- **Recruitment Status Dot:** Pulsing micro-dot (6px) with an outer translucent ring. Green (`#10B981`) denotes "Open for Auditions", Amber (`#F59E0B`) denotes "Waitlist Only", Slate (`#94A3B8`) denotes "Closed".
